# Clover Hollow design notes

Decisions made so far, in Charlotte's words where possible.

## Feel
- "If the OG FarmVille and Stardew Valley had a baby": the ultimate game for the whole generation.
- **Hand-drawn art** (not pixel art): smooth isometric art like FarmVille, with Stardew's warmth and seasonal color.
- Original crop names, mechanics tweaked "here and there". No FarmVille names, logos or art (Zynga/Take-Two own them).

## Core (build first, as the 2009 launch game)
- The farmer walks to every tile and does each action. "That was legendary."
- Plow → plant → harvest → plow again (harvested plots go fallow).
- Basic fruit trees and animals that never die.
- XP levels with titles.
- A welcome package for new players.
- Ribbons are achievements, separate from mastery: yellow → white → red → blue.
- Crop mastery unlocks at level 10, as in the original.

## Monetization (later)
- Free to play and "play to win"; impatient players can buy things.
- Events have goals that unlock gifts. Players who fall short can buy the smaller ones with cash.
- Players who meet every goal themselves get a **trophy/statue** that can't be bought. It shows who truly worked for it.
- Aimed at small occasional spenders, not big spenders. No random paid boxes.

## Order
Core game → hand-drawn art pass → accounts and friends → neighbors (visit, fertilize, gifts) → storage, coops and stables, collections → crafting and vehicles → seasonal events.
