Privacy note

Clover Hollow · last updated 15 October 2026

Clover Hollow is a hobby game made by Charlotte (GitHub: Crealotte) for friends and testers. This note explains, in plain words, what the game stores about you.

If you don't sign in

Your farm is saved only in this browser on this device. Nothing about your farm is sent to us.

If you sign in

We store:

  • your email address, so we can email you a sign-in link
  • your farmer name and a random friend code
  • your farm save (crops, coins, level and so on)
  • the neighbors you add
  • the technical details our database host logs for security, such as your IP address and when you signed in

We use this only to run the online features you asked for: signing in, saving your farm online and playing with neighbors. There are no ads and no tracking, and we never sell or share your data.

Who can see it

Neighbors you add (and who add you) can see your farmer name, your level and your farm. Nobody else in the game can. Other players never see your email address.

Where it is kept

Online data is stored with Supabase on servers in the EU (Frankfurt). The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Fonts come from Google Fonts and the sign-in code from jsDelivr. Like any website, these services see your IP address when your browser loads files from them.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Testing may end one day. If it does, we will delete all accounts.

Your rights

You can delete your account at any time in the game: Friends → Delete my account. This removes your email, farmer name, friend code, online farm and neighbor links right away. You can also ask to see or correct your data by contacting Charlotte on GitHub. If you are unhappy with how your data is handled, you can complain to your country's data protection authority.
